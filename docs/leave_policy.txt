Leave Policy
Every employee gets 20 days of paid vacation per year. Vacation requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance and are approved by your manager.
Sick leave: employees get 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is needed if you are away for more than three days in a row.
Parental leave: new parents get 16 weeks of paid leave. It can be taken any time within the first year.
